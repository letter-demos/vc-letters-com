VC Letters Onboarding Guide for Founders

A complete guide to getting started with VC Letters: workspace setup, templates, contacts, collaboration, and send tracking.

Who this guide is for

This guide is for early-stage founders who want to move from a blank page to a credible, tracked investor letter. A condensed version is available as the Onboarding Checklist at /blog/onboarding-checklist.

Step 1: Set up your workspace

Add your company name, logo, and sending address. Write a default sign-off and a two-sentence boilerplate describing what you build. These appear in every template so you never retype them.

Step 2: Choose a template

The investor update template includes blocks for highlights, metrics, asks, and lowlights. The intro request template is short and forwardable. The outreach template leads with traction and a clear single ask.

Step 3: Import and organize contacts

Upload a CSV with name, firm, and email. Tag investors by stage and sector so you can send relevant letters to the right groups. Duplicates are flagged before import completes.

Step 4: Collaborate

Invite cofounders as editors and advisors as viewers or commenters. Only workspace owners can approve a letter for sending.

Step 5: Send and track

Turn on send tracking, send a test to yourself, then send to your group. The activity timeline shows opens, replies, and forwards, and you can set a reminder to follow up after a few days.
