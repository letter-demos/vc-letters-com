About VC Letters

VC Letters began in 2022 when Hannah Boyle, a former startup operations lead, watched three friends spend weeks on fundraising emails that went unanswered. The problem was rarely the business. It was a long, unfocused message sent to the wrong person at the wrong time.

Hannah teamed up with engineer Tomás Kerrigan, who had built reporting tools at a small payments company. They interviewed over 80 founders and investors and collected what the replies had in common: a clear ask, one strong metric, and a message short enough to read on a phone.

Today VC Letters is a team of nine working remotely from Pittsburgh and beyond. We stay deliberately small and focused on one job: helping founders communicate with the people who back them.

What we value

  • Be brief and be specific
  • Honest updates build lasting trust
  • Founders own their relationships, so we never email on their behalf without consent
  • Make good habits easy
