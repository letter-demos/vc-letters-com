The anatomy of a monthly investor update that gets replies

Most investors read updates on their phone between meetings. That means your update has about thirty seconds to show them where you are and how they can help. A reliable structure helps.

Start with a two-line summary: how the month went and the single number that matters most. Follow with three wins, one honest miss, and your current runway. Investors trust founders who name the miss plainly.

Close with specific asks. Instead of writing that you would welcome intros, say you are looking for a head of sales with marketplace experience and name the kind of person. Specific asks get specific replies.

Finally, keep a steady cadence. A short update sent on the same day each month builds more confidence than a long one sent whenever there is good news.
