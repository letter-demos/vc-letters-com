How to ask for a warm intro without burdening your connector

A warm intro depends on a busy person doing you a favor. Your job is to make that favor nearly effortless.

Write a forwardable blurb of four or five sentences that the connector can paste into a new email. Include what the company does, one traction point, what you are raising, and why this investor in particular.

Give the connector an easy exit. A line such as 'No worries if this is not a fit' keeps the relationship comfortable and often makes people more willing to help.

Follow up once after about a week, and thank them whether or not the meeting happens. Connectors remember founders who are gracious.
