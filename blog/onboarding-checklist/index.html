Onboarding Checklist for New VC Letters Workspaces

A practical checklist for early-stage founders setting up VC Letters: workspace, templates, contacts, and a first tracked send.

Before you start

This checklist is for early-stage founders setting up VC Letters for the first time. Plan for about an hour. You will need your company one-liner, your latest key metrics, and a list of investors in a spreadsheet.

The checklist

1. Create your workspace and add your company name, logo, and sending address.

2. Set your default sign-off and a short company boilerplate so every letter starts consistent.

3. Pick a template: investor update, intro request, or outreach letter.

4. Import contacts from a CSV and tag each by stage and sector.

5. Invite cofounders or advisors and assign editor or viewer roles.

6. Draft your first letter in the editor and use the preview to check formatting.

7. Send to yourself as a test, then send to a small group with tracking on.

After the first send

Check the activity timeline after 48 hours. Follow up on opens without replies with a one-line note. For a fuller explanation of each step, see the Onboarding Guide at /resources/onboarding-guide.
