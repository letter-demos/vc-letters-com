Why you should send fundraising outreach in waves

It is tempting to send every outreach email on the same morning. The trouble is that you cannot fix what you learn afterward.

Instead, divide your list into three waves. Send the first to investors who are a decent fit but not your first choice. Watch which subject lines are opened and which parts of the pitch prompt questions.

Adjust before wave two. You may find that your traction line should come earlier, or that the ask is unclear. By wave three, your strongest targets receive your sharpest letter.

Space the waves a few days apart so meetings cluster in the same few weeks. That creates natural momentum without manufactured pressure.
