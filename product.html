Product

VC Letters helps early-stage founders draft, format, and send investor updates, intro requests, and fundraising outreach letters. Our templates, editor, and send-tracking tools turn a blank page into a clear, credible message in minutes, so founders spend less time writing and more time building.

Update Studio

A guided editor for monthly and quarterly investor updates that pulls your key numbers into a clean, skimmable format.

  • Sections for metrics, wins, misses, asks, and hiring
  • Connects to Stripe and Google Sheets for MRR, burn, and runway
  • Month-over-month deltas added automatically
  • Saves a private archive of every update you have sent

Intro Desk

Writes forwardable intro requests that make it easy for a mutual connection to say yes in under a minute.

  • Generates a short blurb your connector can paste straight into an email
  • Tone options from formal to casual
  • Reminder nudges after 5 and 10 days
  • Tracks which intros turned into meetings

Outreach Sequencer

Plan a fundraising round as a set of personalized letters and follow-ups sent in waves, not all at once.

  • Investor list import from CSV
  • Personalization prompts based on a firm's recent deals and thesis
  • Sends from your Gmail or Outlook account
  • Open, reply, and forward tracking in one pipeline view
